Scales the computed multiplierSOI of every body. Values below 1 shrink SOIs — useful when planets are tightly packed.
Gap Fill Factor 0.45
Fraction of the orbital gap that the SOI may occupy. Lower = smaller SOIs relative to spacing.
Min SOI Radius (× body radius) 4.0×
SOI is always at least this many body-radii wide. Prevents tiny, unenterable SOIs.
Max SOI (fraction of SMA) 0.45
SOI is capped at this fraction of the body's semi-major axis. Prevents overlapping SOIs.
